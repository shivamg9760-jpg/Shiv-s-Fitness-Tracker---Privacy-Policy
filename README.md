# Shiv-s-Fitness-Tracker---Privacy-Policy

**Effective date: September 19, 2026**

Fitness Tracker by Shiv respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information.

## Information Collection

Fitness Tracker by Shiv does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or store personal information on external servers.

The app does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, phone number, location, or other identifying information.

## Fitness Information

The app may display or allow users to enter fitness-related information, such as activities, goals, steps, calories, heart rate, or sleep information.

This information remains within the app on the user’s device. It is not transmitted to the developer or to any third party.

## Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Fitness Tracker by Shiv does not:

* Use advertising services
* Use third-party analytics services
* Track users across apps or websites
* Collect advertising identifiers
* Sell or share user information

## Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party services that collect user information.

## Children’s Privacy

Fitness Tracker by Shiv does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not collect or transmit personal information, no children’s personal information is sent to the developer.

## Data Security

The app does not maintain an external database or server containing user information. Any information entered into the app remains under the user’s control on their device.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app’s features or information-handling practices change. Any updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
